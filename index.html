About Me

Self-taught.
Impact-driven.
Always Evolving.

I’m a self-taught Graphic Designer and Visual Storyteller with over 11.5 years of experience creating impactful visuals for digital platforms, marketing campaigns, and brand communication. My journey has been shaped by hands-on learning, real-world experience, and a deep passion for creativity.

I specialize in social media creatives, emailers, branding, campaign visuals, and presentation design, transforming ideas into visuals that capture attention, communicate clearly, and deliver results. My approach combines creative thinking with strategic design, ensuring every visual is both aesthetically strong and purpose-driven.

I also leverage AI-powered tools to enhance ideation, streamline workflows, and improve efficiency - blending technology with creativity to deliver thoughtful, high-quality designs that perform.

Design, for me, is about creating visuals that connect, inspire, and leave a lasting impact.

I use AI tools to enhance creativity, speed up workflows, and explore design possibilities more efficiently. These tools support my process while ensuring the final output remains thoughtful, strategic, and aligned with brand goals.
